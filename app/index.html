    Roadmap to the platinum

    Play in this order. The story takes 60–80 hours (PowerPyx); the 100% cleanup after it takes about 100 more; side quests and stragglers about 10. One playthrough is enough — there is free roam after the story, no mission replay and no New Game+.

    Missable timeline

    Only one trophy can be missed, and only in Chapter 1. Everything else stays available in free roam after the story — but there is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter (15 manual slots).

    Save habits that protect the platinum

    • Keep a manual save at the start of each chapter and one before the final mission.
    • Do the Chapter 1 meditation the morning after Down to Earth — it never comes back.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off on your trophy save.
    • Optional missions with black icons and pink letters can disappear after a while — they do not matter for trophies, but do them when they appear anyway.
